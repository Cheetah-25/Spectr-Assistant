## Methods worth testing

- **MIR ATR** (Main): ATR probes measure water in oil at a distance over a mid-IR fiber, and MIR gives well-defined absorption peaks; the water example is the only application of this kind in the sources, so it is the method to test first. [S02 p.13; S01 p.10; S01 p.5; S02 p.6]
- **NIR** (Compare): NIR is the most common online fiber-probe method and suits remote fiber probes, but its broad overlapping bands need chemometrics, and a bioprocess example reports possible lack of sensitivity below 0.1 %. [S01 p.10; S01 p.5; S01 p.16; S02 p.6]
- **Raman** (Only if needed): Raman is complementary to MIR and needs special probes; the pharmaceutical example used 785 nm excitation, with the Raman shift in cm-1 as the axis. Weak signals and fluorescence in many organic compounds make it a fallback only. [S01 p.5; S01 p.14–15; S01 p.11]
- **Fluorescence** (Not suitable): Most chemicals do not fluoresce, and the fused approach was limited to samples with at least one fluorescent component; no fluorescent marker for these targets appears in the sources. [S01 p.5; S04 p.4]
- **UV-Vis** (Only if needed): UV-ATR suits opaque or turbid media, but overlapping signals in real mixtures require chemometrics, and no source links UV-Vis to ester, methanol or water in this matrix. [S01 p.16; S01 p.5]

## Multispectral option

**MIR ATR + NIR**: MIR gives well-defined peaks while NIR bands are broad and overlapping, so the pair may separate features one method blurs; NIR also reaches further into the stream. Mixed-phase media may need more than one method.. Probe: separate synchronised probes. Fusion: low-level fusion of synchronised spectra. Prove the gain: On the same validation samples, compare MIR ATR alone, NIR alone and fused MIR + NIR PLS models for ester, methanol and water against EN 14103, EN 14110 and EN ISO 12937; the combination counts only if it beats the best single method. A combined same-spot probe is needed only if MP-2 proves heterogeneous, since no combined MIR + NIR probe is shown in the sources. [S01 p.5; S01 p.10; S01 p.7; S01 p.8; S01 p.9; S01 p.14–15; S01 p.15; Doc p.7]

- **S01 p.7** (S01_Multispectral_Laboratory.pdf): Complex media (oil, milk, fermentation media, tissue) may need two or more methods; examples: NIR+fluorescence gave more accurate biomass in yeast fermentation and distinguished intra- and extracellular metabolites; NIR+IR showed benefits for tumour boundaries; fluorescence+IR was needed to classify tissues; NIR+Raman gave the best results for pellet coating.
- **S01 p.15** (S01_Multispectral_Laboratory.pdf): The cited feed-frame study reports that fused NIR+Raman PLS models predicted API potency more accurately than single-method models (in that study).
